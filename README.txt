Monitor your FireBoard directly from Homey.

This app connects to your FireBoard Cloud account and provides live temperatures, battery status and device information directly inside Homey.

Features

• Live temperatures for up to 6 probes
• Internal board temperature
• Battery level monitoring
• Automatic probe name synchronization
• Online/offline detection
• Configurable polling interval
• Homey Flow support
• Support for FireBoard 2 and FireBoard 2 Drive

Requirements

• A FireBoard Cloud account
• A supported FireBoard device

Supported devices

• FireBoard 2
• FireBoard 2 Drive


This app is an independent Homey integration and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FireBoard Labs.
