\# 🔥 FireBoard for Homey



> \*\*The Complete Package.\*\*



A complete Homey integration for FireBoard cloud-connected thermometers.



Monitor your FireBoard directly from Homey with live temperatures, dynamic probe names, battery status, device health monitoring and Homey Flow support.



\---



\## Features



\### 🌡️ Live Temperature Monitoring



\* Live probe temperatures

\* Internal FireBoard temperature

\* Battery level

\* Automatic probe detection



\### 🏷️ Dynamic Probe Names



Probe names are synchronized directly from your FireBoard and automatically updated inside Homey.



\### 📶 Online / Offline Detection



The app automatically detects when your FireBoard is no longer sending live data and marks the device as unavailable.



\### ⚙️ Configurable Polling



Choose the polling interval that best suits your setup.



Supported intervals:



\* 15 seconds

\* 30 seconds

\* 60 seconds \*(default)\*

\* 120 seconds

\* 300 seconds



\### 🌬️ FireBoard Drive Ready



The app detects FireBoard Drive hardware and includes the foundation for future Drive functionality.

\# Installation



1\. Install the FireBoard app on Homey.

2\. Add a new FireBoard device.

3\. Sign in using your FireBoard Cloud account.

4\. Select your FireBoard device.

5\. Start monitoring your cook.



\---



\# Privacy



This application communicates directly with the official FireBoard Cloud API.



Your FireBoard credentials are only used for authentication with FireBoard Cloud and are never shared with third parties.
